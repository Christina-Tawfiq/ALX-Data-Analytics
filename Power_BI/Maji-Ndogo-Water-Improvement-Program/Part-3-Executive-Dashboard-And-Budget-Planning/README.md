# 📈 Part 3: Executive Dashboard & Budget Planning

## 📌 Overview

This phase focused on transforming analytical findings into decision-support tools for policymakers and stakeholders across Maji Ndogo.

Using Power BI, executive dashboards were developed to estimate infrastructure investment requirements, prioritize improvement projects, allocate budgets, and measure the potential impact of proposed interventions.

**Goal: transform analytical insights into actionable planning and investment decisions.**

---

## 💰 Business Problem

Decision-makers needed answers to critical planning questions:

- How much investment is required to improve water accessibility?
- Which provinces should be prioritized first?
- What types of improvements are most needed?
- How should budgets be allocated?
- Which interventions would deliver the greatest impact?

The objective was to create executive-level dashboards that support strategic infrastructure planning and resource allocation.

---

## 🌍 National Executive Dashboard

### Visuals

- Basic Water Access KPI
- Improvement Potential KPI
- Total Upgrade Cost KPI
- Provincial Comparison
- Budget Allocation by Province
- Required Improvements by Type
- Population Distribution
- Rural vs Urban Analysis

### Key Insights

- Current Basic Water Access across Maji Ndogo is approximately **34%**.
- Planned infrastructure improvements could increase access by approximately **66%**.
- The estimated national upgrade budget is approximately **$146.7M**.
- Kilimani requires the highest number of planned improvements (**6.7K improvements**).
- Sokoto receives the largest budget allocation (**$40.15M**).
- Amanzi requires the fewest planned improvements (**3.7K improvements**).
- Water treatment initiatives represent the largest infrastructure improvement category nationwide.
- Install RO Filters account for the largest improvement requirement (**7.1K planned installations**).

### Analysis Outcome

The executive dashboard provides a national planning framework that allows stakeholders to evaluate investment priorities, compare provinces, and estimate the impact of infrastructure upgrades.

---

## 🟠 Sokoto Province Dashboard

### Key Insights

- Basic Water Access: **31%**
- Improvement Potential: **69%**
- Total Upgrade Cost: **$40.15M**
- Approximately **69%** of residents live in rural communities.
- Drill wells represent the largest investment category.
- Rural communities receive nearly **80%** of the planned provincial budget.
- More than **1,700 wells** are required to improve accessibility.

### Analysis Outcome

Sokoto's primary challenge is infrastructure availability, requiring significant investment in well construction and rural service expansion.

---

## 🔵 Kilimani Province Dashboard

### Key Insights

- Basic Water Access: **28%**
- Improvement Potential: **72%**
- Total Upgrade Cost: **$39.25M**
- Kilimani has the lowest current access level among all provinces.
- Public tap expansion represents the largest budget category.
- Water treatment solutions dominate improvement requirements.
- The province requires the highest number of planned improvements.

### Analysis Outcome

Kilimani presents the greatest opportunity for improvement and should be considered a high-priority investment area.

---

## 🩷 Hawassa Province Dashboard

### Key Insights

- Basic Water Access: **30%**
- Improvement Potential: **70%**
- Total Upgrade Cost: **$22.55M**
- Approximately **74%** of residents live in rural areas.
- Water treatment projects account for the majority of planned improvements.
- Relatively few new wells are required compared to other provinces.

### Analysis Outcome

Improving water quality would have a greater impact than expanding infrastructure availability in Hawassa.

---

## 🔷 Akatsi Province Dashboard

### Key Insights

- Basic Water Access: **37%**
- Improvement Potential: **63%**
- Total Upgrade Cost: **$31.35M**
- Approximately **77%** of residents live in rural communities.
- RO Filter installations represent the largest improvement category.
- Public tap expansion remains a significant investment area.
- Water quality improvements are a major priority.

### Analysis Outcome

Akatsi requires targeted investment in water treatment technologies to improve access to safe drinking water.

---

## 🟣 Amanzi Province Dashboard

### Key Insights

- Basic Water Access: **41%**
- Improvement Potential: **59%**
- Total Upgrade Cost: **$13.43M**
- Amanzi has the highest existing access level among all provinces.
- Urban residents represent the majority of the population.
- Infrastructure repair is the most common improvement requirement.
- The province requires the lowest level of investment.

### Analysis Outcome

Amanzi demonstrates stronger service performance than other provinces and requires targeted optimization rather than major expansion projects.

---

## 💡 Strategic Findings

- Different provinces require different intervention strategies.
- Rural communities account for the majority of planned investment.
- Water treatment improvements represent the largest national priority.
- Infrastructure repair remains a recurring challenge across provinces.
- Budget allocation should consider both population impact and accessibility gaps.
- Provincial dashboards enable more effective local-level planning and prioritization.

---

## ✅ Part 3 Takeaway

This phase transformed operational and analytical findings into a practical planning framework.

By combining KPIs, budgeting analysis, improvement tracking, and provincial reporting, decision-makers can identify where investment will deliver the greatest impact and create sustainable improvements in water accessibility across Maji Ndogo.
